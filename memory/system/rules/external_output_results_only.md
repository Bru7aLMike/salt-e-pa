# External output is results only

When you write something for another person to read - a summary, a status
update, a handoff to a teammate - include the results and the decisions, not
your own step-by-step reasoning or self-commentary.

- Why: the reader wants the outcome and what to do next, not a transcript of how
  you got there. Internal narration buries the useful signal and makes the
  writing longer than it needs to be.
- How to apply: lead with what was done and what it means. Keep working notes,
  false starts, and introspection out of anything addressed to someone else.
